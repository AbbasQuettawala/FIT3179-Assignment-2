# Hand-sketch guide

The rubric awards zero for the sketch if it is digitally created. Draw this plan physically on paper, annotate it by hand, scan it, and export it as a PDF.

The sketch is worth 2 of the assignment's 25 unit marks: 8% of the assignment grade. Abbas confirmed on 6 October that it is not finished. Ask Bruno about reviewing it now; do not claim it was discussed earlier.

## Sheet 1 — whole-page architecture

Start with one legible hand-drawn A4 overview containing these four clearly labelled chapters. Additional detail sheets are optional, not a requirement to produce five pages:

1. **The great unbundling** — hero waffle, horizon chart, connected scatterplot, video-service heatmap
2. **Different age groups** — parallel coordinates, bullet chart, bump chart, slopegraph
3. **Ears, eyes and trust** — audio heatmap, bipartite network, news-reach dot plot and separately attributed trust prose
4. **Access sets the ceiling** — inclusion choropleth, population Dorling cartogram, metro–regional dumbbell, DAB+ proportional symbols, AM/FM site dots, conclusion

Draw the top navigation, section colour changes, source lines and card boundaries. Include the age, video-format, audio-format and linked state selectors. Show the horizon colour key and the cartogram's one-million-residents area key. Use coloured pencils for the palette: forest green, mint, magenta, warm yellow and cream.

## Optional detail sheets

For each chapter, draw every chart with plausible axes, labels, annotations, legends and hover notes. Add arrows showing the intended reading order and handwritten notes explaining:

- the insight each visual communicates;
- why its idiom is appropriate;
- what interaction is available;
- the source and reference year;
- how small samples or non-comparable measures are disclosed.

For the access chapter, make the four map idioms unmistakably different. Label what colour, area and position mean. Show the city-view controls on the radio maps and note that licensed sites are not audiences or coverage. Sketch explanatory annotation paragraphs: describe a pattern's significance and limits rather than copying its highlighted value.

## Required submission steps

1. Write your name and the actual drawing date on each page. Keep your student ID in the submission template rather than the public sketch unless your tutor specifically requires it there.
2. Photograph or scan all pages clearly.
3. Combine them into one PDF.
4. Upload the PDF somewhere publicly accessible.
5. Put the public PDF URL and a screenshot of the sketch into the assignment template.

The brief expects a hand-drawn A4 design discussed with your tutor in Weeks 7–8. If you have not completed that stage, discuss the timing honestly with your tutor. Date any new sketch accurately and describe it as a revised design; do not present it as an earlier sketch. The submitted sketch and final page need to match in topic and structure.
